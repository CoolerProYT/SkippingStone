# Getting started

Skipping Stone adds stones you find along shorelines and throw across water. How far they go depends on the stone, your timing and your aim.

## Install

1. Install [Fabric](https://fabricmc.net/) with Fabric API, or [NeoForge](https://neoforged.net/), for Minecraft **26.1.2**.
2. Install [CoolerConfig](https://github.com/CoolerProYT/CoolerConfig). Skipping Stone needs it and will not load without it.
3. Put the Skipping Stone jar in your `mods` folder.

## Your first throw

**1. Find a shoreline.** Look for sand, red sand or gravel with water right next to it, like the edge of a river or a beach.

**2. Pick up a stone.** With an empty hand, right-click the block. You get a skipping stone and the block sinks a little, becoming a [sifted block](./finding-stones#sifted-blocks). Each block only gives one stone.

<ItemSlot id="skippingstone:skipping_stone_chipped" name="Skipping Stone" label />

**3. Face the water and hold right-click.** A power meter takes over your experience bar. A marker sweeps across it: red, yellow, green, yellow, red, and back.

**4. Let go while the marker is in the green.** The stone flies out and skips across the water. The number of skips counts up above your hotbar, and the result shows when the stone sinks.

::: tip
Aim a little below the horizon, about where the water meets the far shore. Looking up lobs the stone and it sinks on impact.
:::
