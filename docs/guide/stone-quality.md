# Stone quality

Every skipping stone has one of four qualities. It is set when you pick the stone up and shown in its tooltip.

Better stones are a trade-off: they fly faster, but the green zone on the [power meter](./throwing) shrinks, so they are harder to throw well.

<TierTable />

The results assume a perfect aim. See [the math](./the-math) for how aim, timing and quality combine.

## What quality changes

- **Speed.** The stone's speed multiplies the power of your throw. The first skip is longer, and since each skip is a fixed fraction of the one before, a longer first skip also means more skips in total.
- **Green zone width.** The green zone narrows evenly from the worst stone to the best. A Perfect stone's green zone is almost four times smaller than a Chipped one's.

Quality does not change the red or yellow zones. A red release sinks whatever the stone.

::: tip Even a miss pays off
A Perfect stone released in the yellow still goes about as far as a Chipped stone released in the green.
:::

## Getting better stones

Quality is rolled from odds that depend on the biome. See [Finding stones](./finding-stones#which-stone-you-get) for the full table. Stones of different qualities do not stack together.
