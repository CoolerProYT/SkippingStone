# FAQ

## Right-clicking sand does nothing

Check that:

- Your main hand is empty.
- There is water right beside the block, on the same level.
- There is no water on top of the block. Underwater sand and gravel never give stones.
- The block has not already been picked. Sifted blocks sit a little lower than normal ones.

## My stone sank straight away

Either the marker was in the red, or your aim was too far off. Look slightly down at the water, not up at the sky. See [Throwing](./guide/throwing#aiming).

## Do I lose the stone?

Only if it sinks. If it hits land or a mob, it drops as an item where it landed.

## My experience bar disappeared

The power meter covers the experience bar only while you are holding right-click with a stone. It comes back as soon as you let go.

## Why do my stones not stack?

Stones only stack with stones of the same quality.

## No firework when I set a record

Your very first counted throw sets a record without a firework, since there was nothing to beat. Red releases never count.

## Where are records stored?

In the world save, in `data/skippingstone/skip_records.dat`. They are separate for every world. The Statistics screen and scoreboard objectives are copies kept in sync with it.

## Does it need to be on the server?

Yes. Install it on both the server and every client, together with CoolerConfig.
